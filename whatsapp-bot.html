Why pair a second number?

When you link a number to the bot, it takes over that WhatsApp account. If you pair your main number, you can't use WhatsApp on your phone the same way.

Recommended: Get a second SIM (₦100-500) or use a virtual number. Run the bot on that number. Keep your main line for personal chats.
