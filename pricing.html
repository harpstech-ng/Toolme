Questions

How do I send emails with my own account?

After subscribing, you connect your own free Brevo account inside the mailer. Your emails send through your Brevo, using your quota. We never touch your sending. You are always in control.

What happens when I hit my daily limit?

The tool blocks further sends until midnight. You can upgrade any time to increase your limit instantly. Your unsent recipients stay saved. Just send them tomorrow.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan runs until the end of the current period, then downgrades to Free. No auto-billing traps.

Is my payment secure?

All payments go through Paystack. We never see your card details. Paystack handles everything and confirms when your payment succeeds.

What if I want more than 1,000 emails a day?

Message us on WhatsApp. We have custom plans for agencies and high-volume senders. Rates start at 50,000 Naira per month for 5,000 per day.
